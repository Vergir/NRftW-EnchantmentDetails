# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

* Roll ranges: every rolled enchantment value shows its worst–best range, e.g. `Damage increased by 7% (3–10)`, on item
  tooltips, stored enchantments, previews and exalt pop-ups.
* Detailed enchantment info: the numbers enchantment texts leave out: drain rates, tick intervals, internal cooldowns,
  Low Health / Focus thresholds, sprint time, nearby-enemy cap and radius, refill amounts. Also on gem tooltips and the
  reroll screen.
* Facet numbers: what each facet changes, downside included, e.g. `Durable (+75 Durability, -10% Focus Gain)`; the
  facet keyword pop-up is hidden while this is on.
* Checkboxes for each part in Options > Gameplay.
